Background information In traditional physical machine and virtual machine deployment, calls between various services can be made through fixed IP + port. With the advent of the cloud-native era, enterprise business deployment is more inclined to cloud-native containerization. However, in a containerized environment, the startup and destruction of service instances are very frequent.
