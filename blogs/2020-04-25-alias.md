Description The alias plugin eliminates CNAME records from zone apex by making the subsequent resolved records look like they belong to the zone apex. This behaves similarily to CloudFlare’s Zone Flattening. This plugin works only with plugins that produce A or AAAA records alongside the CNAME record.
