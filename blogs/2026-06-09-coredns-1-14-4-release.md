This release improves transport security and operational flexibility, with enhancements for DoH3 and DoQ, improved DNSSEC signing behavior, and support for the loong64 architecture. It also adds configurable cache verification, hostname resolution for forward targets, incoming connection support for dnstap, fallthrough support in the secondary plugin, automatic zone reloads, and improved forwarding behavior for NODATA responses.
